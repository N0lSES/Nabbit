Download, convert, trim and compress your media, and play your music, all from one window. Made for files you have the right to use.

Try the demo ▸▸ View on GitHub

The demo is Nabbit's real interface running in your browser on sample data: nothing is downloaded or converted.

What's inside06

01

Video downloader

Paste a link, pick the format, resolution and size. Batches too.

02

Music downloader

Songs, albums and playlists from YouTube Music and SoundCloud, tagged with cover art.

03

Converter

Video, audio, images and documents, also from File Explorer's right-click menu.

04

Trimmer

Cut clips on a waveform, keep the parts you want, save frames.

05

Compressor

Shrink videos to a size you choose, like Discord's upload limit.

06

Audio tools & player

Stems, mastering, pitch and tempo; a music player with DJ mode, lyrics and your own Nabbit Home.
